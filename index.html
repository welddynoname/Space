01 / The big picture

What is space?

Space is the vast region beyond Earth's atmosphere. It contains everything from tiny particles to enormous galaxies.

01 — SCALE

Almost endless

The observable universe contains billions of galaxies, each with millions or billions of stars.

02 — ENVIRONMENT

Near-vacuum

There is very little matter in space. Sound needs a medium, so it cannot travel through a vacuum.

03 — MYSTERY

Still expanding

Scientists observe that the universe is expanding. Many questions about it remain unanswered.

02 / Our cosmic neighbourhood

The Solar System

Our Solar System formed around 4.6 billion years ago. The Sun's gravity holds eight planets in orbit.

The four inner planets are rocky. The four outer planets are giant worlds — including gas giants and ice giants.

03 / Our home world

Earth — the blue planet

Earth is our home and the only planet known to support life. Its special conditions make liquid water and complex ecosystems possible.

71%

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water.

1 atmosphere

Our atmosphere supplies essential gases and helps protect life.

Earth is not just a place to explore from — it is a planet we must protect.

04 / Deep space objects

Giants of the universe

The night sky is filled with objects that challenge our imagination.

01 — STARS

Cosmic furnaces

Stars are huge spheres of hot plasma. They produce energy through nuclear fusion. The Sun is our nearest star.

02 — GALAXIES

Island universes

Galaxies are vast systems of stars, gas, dust and dark matter held together by gravity. Our galaxy is the Milky Way.

03 — BLACK HOLES

Gravity extremes

A black hole has gravity so strong that nothing — not even light — can escape from inside its event horizon.

05 / Human exploration

How do we explore space?

Robots and astronauts use advanced technology to study distant worlds and understand our universe.

01 — OBSERVE

Telescopes

They collect light from distant objects. Space telescopes can observe wavelengths blocked by Earth's atmosphere.

02 — CONNECT

Satellites

Satellites support GPS navigation, communication, weather forecasting and scientific research.

03 — DISCOVER

Rovers & probes

Robotic explorers travel to other worlds, take pictures, analyse rocks and send data back to Earth.

06 / Signals from the cosmos

Three facts to blow your mind

FACT 01

A day on Venus

Venus rotates very slowly. One rotation takes longer than one trip around the Sun.

FACT 02

The moving Moon

The Moon is moving away from Earth by about 3.8 centimetres every year.

FACT 03

Look into the past

Light takes time to travel. When we see distant stars, we see them as they were in the past.

The farther away we look, the further back in time we can observe.
